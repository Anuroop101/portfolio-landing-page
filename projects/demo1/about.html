● About Elara Voss

The spaces we live in shape how we feel.

After training in architecture at the Bartlett School and spending four years at a commercial practice, I realised that the spaces I cared most about weren't offices or retail. They were the homes people came back to every evening.

Voss Studio was founded in 2014 with one rule: every project starts with how the client actually lives. Not trends. Not what's in the magazines.
