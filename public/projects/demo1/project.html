● Project Case Study

Kensington Townhouse

A full living room redesign for a young couple looking for a space that felt grounded and warm, avoiding the sterile white-box look of typical London renovations.

The Brief

The existing space had good bones — high ceilings, a working fireplace, original cornicing — but the previous owners had covered everything in white paint and beige carpet. The challenge was to bring the architecture back to life without turning it into a period recreation.

Project Details

Location

Kensington, London

Scope

Full living room

Timeline

14 weeks
